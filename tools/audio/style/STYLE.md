# The sound of Karak

One room: a torch-lit stone dungeon seen from a tabletop. Everything is acoustic and close, as if played at the table.

## Music (Lyria)
- **Instruments**: cello and low strings, viola da gamba, hurdy-gurdy drones, frame drum, dulcimer, lute, low wooden flute, a distant bell. No vocals, no choir, no synth pads.
- **Mood by situation**:
  - *lobby* (start screen, rules, Hall of fame): warm and light, a fire in the tavern before the descent.
  - *explore* (the game): dark but calm, it sits under an hour of play without tiring; two variants alternate.
  - *fight*: taut, drums and low brass, tense rather than epic, short loop.
  - *dragon*: the same fight, bigger and heavier.
  - *stings* (no loop): victory, defeat, the end of the game, Lord Karak's reveal.
- **Loops**: steady dynamics, no outro; the tool cuts the last seconds and crossfades the end into the start.
- **Loudness**: music −18 LUFS, stings −17 LUFS.

## Sounds (ElevenLabs)
- Foley, not synth: wood, stone, iron, cloth, parchment, coins, embers.
- Short and dry, they sit on top of the music without a long reverb tail.
- Frequent sounds (clicks, steps, dice) have 2–3 variants; the game picks one at random.
- **Loudness**: −16 LUFS, peaks limited to −1 dBFS.
