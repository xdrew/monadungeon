# Regent hero turns closed too early: Tireless after a spell-assisted win, Markul's retreat choice

**Date:** 2026-10-15 11:31
**Files:** src/src/Game/Battle/Battle.php, fe/src/views/GameView.vue, fe/src/utils/battleUtils.js, src/tests/Game/Battle/RegentHeroBattleTest.php
**Severity:** high

## Problem

Two Karak: Regent hero rules worked in PHPUnit but not in the game:

1. Valduk "Tireless" after a fight won with spells: the "Win, leave it and keep moving" button finalized the battle, the turn was already over, and `/game/tireless` failed (not your turn).
2. Baron Markul's retreat after a lost Lightning Attack: the retreat panel never appeared; the client ended the turn and the backend auto-retreated him to the first option.

## Root Cause

1. `Battle::finalizeBattle` dispatches `EndTurn` after every finalized fight, wins included (only Unstoppable and Reincarnation were exempt). The new Tireless path was tested against a Field state seeded by hand, never through the real finalization, so the early `EndTurn` was invisible.
2. `GameView::handleFinalizeBattle` predicted the result with `monster.hp`, but `battleInfo.monster` is the monster's HP as a number (`Field::lastBattleInfo['monster']`). Both comparisons were always false, so every finalization was predicted as a win and the client called `endTurn`. Before Regent this was harmless (the backend had already ended lost turns); a turn that must stay open after a loss exposed it. The prediction also ignored spells chosen in the modal.

## Solution

1. `finalizeBattle` keeps the turn open for a won finalization without pickup when the hero is `tireless()`; the client still ends it for the plain "leave the reward" choice. Regression tests drive `Battle::start` + `finalizeBattle` with a Frost Fist (Valduk: no `EndTurn`; another hero: `EndTurn`).
2. New `predictBattleResult(totalDamage, monster, tieIsWin)` in `fe/src/utils/battleUtils.js` accepts the numeric HP; `handleFinalizeBattle` decides on `endTurn` / Tireless from the backend's `finalTotalDamage`, not the pre-spell prediction. `[FIX]` log of predicted vs final result. Regression check: a node script calling the helper (3 vs 5 HP → loose; previously the inline expression returned win).

## Prevention

- Test a "the turn stays open" rule through the command that really runs (finalize, then the follow-up action), not against a hand-seeded aggregate state.
- When a backend payload field is a scalar (`monster` = HP), do not read properties off it; centralise such predicates in a util that can be run with node.
- Any client-side `endTurn` after a battle is a second authority on the turn: prefer the backend's final numbers (`finalTotalDamage`) over a local prediction.
