# Regent spells treated as treasures in post-battle slot checks and the pickup prompt

**Date:** 2026-10-15 10:40
**Files:** fe/src/utils/itemUtils.js, fe/src/utils/fieldUtils.js, fe/src/views/GameView.vue
**Severity:** medium

## Problem

With three spells in the inventory, beating a Skeleton Mage (Frost Fist) or a Giant Bat (Dark Thorn) after spending spells in the fight showed the wrong replacement list: `GameView.vue` mapped the reward to the `treasures` category, and the free-slot check ignored the spells consumed in that fight. Separately, `isItemWorthPickingUp` prompted to pick up a Frost Fist even when the slots were full and the hero already held one.

## Root Cause

Spell types were spelled out as literal lists (`['fireball', 'teleport']`, `rewardType === 'fireball' || rewardType === 'teleport'`) in several frontend files. The Karak: Regent work extended the lists it touched (damage maps, inventory limits, category maps), but missed two copies that were not in the plan's file list, so anything not in the list fell through to the treasure/default branch.

## Solution

Added `SPELL_TYPES` to `fe/src/utils/itemUtils.js` (fireball, teleport, frost_fist, dark_thorn) and used it in the GameView slot check, the GameView replacement category and `isItemWorthPickingUp`. `[FIX]` logs report the slot decision and the full-slot pickup decision. Regression check: a Node script bundled with esbuild calling `isItemWorthPickingUp` with full slots and a held Frost Fist (failed before, passes after).

## Prevention

- When adding an item type, grep the frontend for every literal list of existing types of the same category (`'fireball'`, `'teleport'`) and route them through a shared constant.
- A default branch that silently means "treasure" hides missing cases; prefer explicit category lookup from one place.
- A frontend unit-test runner would let such helpers be covered directly; today only Playwright exists.
