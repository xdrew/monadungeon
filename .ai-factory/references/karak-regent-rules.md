# Karak: Regent — rules digest

Source: `Karak Regent.pdf` in the repo root (a scan without a text layer; read page images once on 2026-10-15, do not re-read — this digest is the reference). Pages 1–5 are rules, 6–7 are art/cover.

Expansion adds: 4 heroes, 2 monsters, 2 spells, 2 room kinds (18 tiles: 6 arenas, 4 cursed rooms, 8 rooms), 8 monster tokens (6 giant bats, 2 skeleton mages), and the optional "Lord Karak" game variant. Components are mixed into the base game.

## Lord Karak rooms
**Battle Arena** — on the FIRST entry the entering player chooses another player to duel and that player's figure is moved onto the arena. Duel = monster fight where the strength to beat is the other hero's total attack. The entering player decides first (spells/abilities), then the summoned player. Winner takes from the loser one treasure or any item/spell; loser loses 1 HP. Tie: nothing. Both figures stay on the arena afterwards. Arena activates only once; later it is a normal corridor. All combat abilities apply (Warrior reroll, Thief wins ties, Oracle +1 when the arena is her first step or when she was summoned, …); a cursed hero cannot use abilities.
**Cursed Room** — every entering player rolls the dice: 1–3 → Curse of Karak on the hero (take the token), may continue the remaining moves; 4–6 → avoided. Already cursed → no roll. Active for the whole game. Note: a hero forced back onto the cursed room after losing a duel with a monster on the adjacent tile rolls again.

## New spells
**Dark Thorn** (from the Giant Bat) — on the owner's turn, choose another hero, they lose 1 HP; removed from the game after use; costs no move; several per turn, even on the same hero. If Horan loses his last HP to it, Reincarnation applies as usual.
**Frost Fist** (from the Skeleton/Ice Mage) — +2 attack in a fight; discarded (removed from the game) after use; usable after the dice roll; several per fight, combinable with other spells. The Wizard keeps it like the fireball.

## New monsters (tokens)
Giant Bat — 6 HP (6 tokens), drops Dark Thorn. Skeleton Mage — 11 HP (2 tokens), drops Frost Fist.

## New heroes
**Valduk, the Barbarian** — *Berserk*: at exactly 3–4 HP +1 attack, at 1–2 HP +2. When healing he may choose how many HP to heal. *Tireless*: if he defeats a monster and does not take the loot, he may continue his remaining moves.
**Baron Markul, the Battle Mage** — *Sword Master*: a sword has strength 3 in his hands. *Lightning Attack*: for all 4 moves, teleport to any tile with a monster and fight it; if he loses he chooses where to retreat, only onto already-open tiles.
**Hanna, the Acrobat** — *Dagger Throwing*: may place daggers (strength 1) in her spell slots; every dagger on her board adds +1 attack; daggers are not consumed. (When cursed, the daggers stay in the slots but she can neither use them nor add new ones.) *Sprint*: instead of normal movement, declare at the start of the turn a run of up to 8 tiles, but she may not enter undiscovered tiles.
**Lady Lorraine, the Ranger** — *Bear Attack*: a die showing 1 counts as 6. *Listening*: for one action she may explore an adjacent tile (place it, draw the monster token) without entering; entering later costs another action; may listen several times per turn. If she discovers an arena by listening and does not enter, the arena stays not activated.

## Lord Karak variant (optional)
Lord Karak enters when all five Lord Karak rooms (arenas + cursed rooms) are discovered. The weakest hero becomes him: 1) fewest treasures; 2) tie → lowest total weapon strength; 3) tie → fewest other items; 4) tie → lowest die roll (reroll ties). That player swaps figure and card: fully healed, curse removed, loses the original abilities (exception: with 2 players he keeps them), gains the abilities of all other heroes in the game. Otherwise the same rules (4 moves, can be cursed, collects treasure); the game still ends with the dragon. If the fifth room is an arena, the transformation is evaluated first, then the arena fight.
