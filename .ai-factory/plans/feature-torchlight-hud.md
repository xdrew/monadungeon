## Settings
- Testing: no (no FE unit runner; verification = `npm run build`, ESLint on touched files, screenshots in the host Chrome in a new tab at `http://127.0.0.1:18080`, the user's e2e run)
- Logging: standard (`console.debug('[hud] …')` only on game-state changes — turn order recomputed, journal opened; nothing per render)
- Docs: yes (README "Theme" section, CLAUDE.md theme bullet)

Follow-up answers (2026-10-15): party = **turn-order rail always** (one layout for 1–5+ players; "Your pack" bottom left; another hero's kit in a popover from their medallion); action rings = **contextual** (the current hero's skills, heal limit, Journal, End Turn; spells are cast by clicking a pack slot; off-turn the rings are dimmed with "Your turn comes after …"); mobile = **field full screen** (top strip of mini medallions, bottom bar of pack + rings, chronicle behind the Journal ring, field padding fixed in CSS and JS); settings as in parts 1–2.

Design reference: canvas https://claude.ai/artifact/BqzwkXjLNihxQtpXrK8dvX, boards "C · Torchlight" (`project/Torchlight.dc.html`: banner, chronicle, action rings, medallions with HP pips and slots) and "C · Five players" (`project/TorchlightFive.dc.html`: "Order of the turn" rail with numerals, "Your pack", dimmed rings with "Your turn comes after Taia and Lorraine"). This part owns everything on the game screen outside `.game-field` except the battle screen, modals/dialogs, the finished banner and leaderboard (part 4), the lobby and rules (part 5). The clash box drawn on the field in board C is part 4.

## Requirements Reconciliation
Authority: user answers > canvas boards > existing code. The e2e contract (`fe/e2e/utils/game-helpers.ts`, `fe/e2e/tests/two-player-game.spec.ts`) must keep passing unchanged.
