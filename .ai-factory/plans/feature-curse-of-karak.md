# Implementation Plan: The Curse of Karak — curse token, skill-less heroes, Portal of Healing

Branch: feature/curse-of-karak (created from feature/oracle-hero @ 8d069e0; hero branches are merged to main together)
Created: 2026-10-14

## Original Request
The Curse of Karak — after the Oracle, same pattern as .ai-factory/plans/feature-oracle-hero.md. Rulebook (a4-karak-rulebook.pdf): "If any player defeats a mummy, one player becomes cursed. The player who defeated the mummy chooses another player and places the curse token on their inventory sheet. The player whose hero was cursed and has the curse token on their hero's card cannot use their hero's skills. Thus the Warrior can roll the dice only once and dies just like the others, the Swordsman can roll 1 and his turn ends after any combat, etc. The curse can be lifted by finishing one's turn on a tile with a HF and removing the curse token along with restoring all HPs. The curse can also be passed on when another mummy is defeated, and the player moves the curse token to another player. However, the token does not have to be moved; it can stay with the previous owner." Also: "Portal of Healing ... can be used either on the caster or on any other player. The target is then moved to any revealed HF and all of their wounds are healed; any curse is removed as well. This does not cost any moves, and it does not end the turn." and the Wizard clause: "if the Wizard is cursed, he has to discard the magic bolt spells during combat just like the others."

Follow-up answers: settings as for the heroes (PHPUnit only, showcase untouched; logging standard; docs yes); the victim is chosen inside the battle modal right after the win; the Portal of Healing is fixed in the same feature (any player as target, heals at once, lifts the curse, does not end the turn).

| A pending choice never blocks the game: `Game::turnEnded` resolves an unmade choice (keep the token if it is in play, otherwise the first other player) before `NextTurn`. No turn action is recorded then (the turn is over). | no stuck games | `CurseOfKarakTest` (g) |
| `Player::$cursed` (new column) is a projection set by `PlayerCursed` / cleared by `CurseLifted`. `Player::getHero()` returns **null while cursed** (every skill check in `Battle`, `Movement`, `Field` and `Player::reduceHP` keeps calling `getHero()`, so all skills switch off without touching the call sites — including the Wizard's Magical Affinity, which is exactly the rulebook's Wizard clause); the new `Player::getChosenHero()` returns the picked hero for display; `Player::isCursed()`. | Lifting: `Game::onPlayerHealedAtFountain(PlayerHealedAtFountain)` clears the token and dispatches `CurseLifted` when the healed player holds it. `Field::onTurnEnded` already dispatches this event for a turn ended on a fountain (and on 0,0), and the Portal of Healing dispatches it for its target, so both rulebook lifts share one path. | rulebook "lifted by finishing one's turn on a HF", "any curse is removed as well" | `CurseOfKarakTest` (h), `PortalOfHealingTest` (b) |
| Portal of Healing (`ItemType::TELEPORT`): `UseSpell` gains `targetPlayerId` (null = caster); target must be a player of this game; `ResetPlayerPosition(target, fountain)`, `ResetPlayerHP(target, afterStun: false)`, `PlayerHealedAtFountain(target, fountain)`, the spell is removed from the **caster**, turn action `USE_SPELL` with `{spell: 'teleport', target, position}`; **no `EndTurn`** — the caster keeps the remaining moves. | rulebook "does not cost any moves, and it does not end the turn" | `PortalOfHealingTest` (a)–(d) |
| `TurnAction::CURSE` ('curse') never counts as a move, is allowed after anything (`Game` validates the pending state) and is skipped by `GameTurn::getLastAction()` like an Unstoppable fight so it does not change what is allowed next; the `pendingItemPickup` guard in `GameTurn::performAction` also lets CURSE through. | keep the turn state machine intact | `GameTurnTest` |
